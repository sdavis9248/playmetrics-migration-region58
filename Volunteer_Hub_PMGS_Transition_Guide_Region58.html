Volunteer Hub & the Governing System
Transition Guide

AYSO Region 58 — Fall 2026 → MY2027  |  Prepared October 26, 2026
← All Region 58 Guides
Bottom Line Region 58 runs two things for its volunteers: the Volunteer Hub at volunteer.ayso58.org, which walks each person through exactly what they owe and does the clicking for them, and the official AYSO record, which until now lived in Sports Connect and the Sports Affinity association site. That official record is moving to the PlayMetrics Governing System (PMGS). The Hub was built so that it does not care where the official record lives — it reads it, explains it, and helps the volunteer close the gaps. This guide proposes how Region 58 moves the Hub's source of truth from Sports Connect and Sports Affinity to PMGS, and settles who does what afterwards so that no requirement is tracked twice and none goes dark. The short version: PMGS becomes the record of authority for registration, background screening, SafeSport and AYSOU completions; the Hub keeps the parts PMGS does not do — role and division candidacy, the exact course for an age group, in-person session sign-up, fingerprinting, referee scheduling, and plain-English guidance.
Who this is for The registrar, the CVPA, division coordinators and the referee administrator, plus anyone at another region evaluating the Hub. The companion Volunteer Compliance Guide explains how PMGS itself works; this page explains how the Hub fits around it. Where this page proposes a step rather than describes a current fact, it says so. Anything marked CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT has not yet been verified in PlayMetrics.

What the Volunteer Hub Is

The Volunteer Hub is Region 58's volunteer-facing website (volunteer.ayso58.org; Region 18 runs the same software at volunteer.ayso18.org). A volunteer signs in with a one-time link sent to their email — there is no password to create, remember or reset — and lands on a single page that answers three questions: what am I signed up for, what do I still owe, and what do I click next? It is one copy of the Hub per region, scoped to that region's volunteers only.

My Compliance — the one-page answer

Shows the volunteer's current-season roles and divisions, their AYSO ID, each requirement with its status (met, in progress, needed, expiring in N days), and a meter such as "4 of 6 shared requirements met — 1 to go, 1 in progress." It is read-only and says so: it reflects the official records on file as of the last sync, which runs several times a day. A background check that has been submitted but not returned shows as in progress, not as missing, so nobody is nagged for something already underway.

Training — the exact course, not a catalog

For each role and division the volunteer declared, the Hub names the one course that satisfies the gap: a 10U coach is told they need the 10U Coach course and that they currently hold 6U and 8U; a 14U referee is told Intermediate grade is required. For online AYSOU courses (Safe Haven, Concussion Awareness, Sudden Cardiac Arrest, Regional Board Member) one click enrolls them. For courses with an in-person field session it lists upcoming sessions nearest first (Region 58's own class outranks a nearer one in another region), enrolls them on request, and lets them switch sessions later. It tracks "online portion done, field session pending" so a half-finished hybrid course is not shown as untouched.

SafeSport and fingerprinting — the two outside websites, explained

SafeSport is not an AYSO University course and LiveScan fingerprinting is not in any system, so these are where volunteers get stuck. The Hub carries a screenshot walkthrough for creating a SafeSport account (including the easy-to-miss "Name of Sport or Community Organization: AYSO" field), the refresher path for renewals, and an upload box for the completion certificate that goes straight to the registrar. For fingerprinting it explains the AYSO LiveScan site, the AYSO-discounted rate at Live Scan Service Centers, what to bring, the ORI number for an outside provider, and asks the volunteer to send their ATI number so the region can record the fingerprinting while the state finishes processing. The reimbursement form is attached.

Register and candidacy — what will you actually do?

A volunteer tells the Hub which roles they want (Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Referee, Youth Referee, Team Manager) and which of the 16 divisions, or "any division." A minor can only choose Youth Referee. A 14U-and-up referee candidacy from someone without an Intermediate grade is recorded but flagged for the referee administrator. This candidacy is what makes the requirements list age-correct, and it feeds team formation: the division coordinators' coach queue and the draft board draw from it.

Nudges and the back office

The Hub emails sign-in links to volunteers who signed up in PlayMetrics but have no official record yet, tells a volunteer when the region has submitted a background-check renewal on their behalf so the provider's email is not a surprise, and lets a volunteer withdraw (which notifies their division coordinator). Everything the Hub shows comes from the region's compliance automation, which also feeds the board-only Region 58 Portal: the same facts, one view per volunteer and one view per division.

What the Hub is not The Hub is not a system of record. It never invents an AYSO ID, a clearance or a certification; it shows what the official system holds and queues requests to change it. For background checks it shows status only (cleared, pending, action needed), never report detail, which is the Fair Credit Reporting Act line the region keeps on the right side of. A request made in the Hub is honest about its state: submitted, processing, done, needs attention.

How the Hub Worked in the Sports Connect Era

Through the Fall 2026 season the official record lived in Sports Connect (the volunteer application) and Sports Affinity (membership, AYSO ID, risk status, certifications), with AYSO University reached only through Sports Connect's single sign-on. Neither has an API, so the Hub's back office drives them the way a registrar would, in a browser, on the volunteer's behalf. That was always a bridge, and most of it retires with those two systems.

What PMGS Does Now

The Volunteer Compliance Guide covers this in full; the essentials for the reconciliation below are these.

  • Registration of record. The region designates each volunteer as staff in the Club System, then Membership → Staff → More Actions → Import Staff → Import from Club OS creates their Governing System membership. The staff title chosen at import sets which requirements apply. Volunteers who sign up through League Volunteer Signups (Beta) follow the same path once accepted (CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT that the beta feeds the import directly).
  • The volunteer's own checklist. In the Member role: photo and waivers, Clearances (SafeSport by entering a U.S. Soccer Federation ID, JDP background screening by clicking Complete Screening), and the Education tab, which opens AYSO University and syncs completions back automatically.
  • Status and expirations. Each member is Not Registered, Pending Member, In Club Review, In Governing Review or Verified; only Verified staff print on official rosters. The Expirations tab flags items 45 days out and emails at 30.
  • What it holds from AYSOU. Safe Haven, Concussion, Sudden Cardiac Arrest, coach licenses and referee grades arrive by sync. SafeSport comes from the Learning Center. Legacy Sports Affinity risk status and certifications were migrated by AYSO National.
  • What it does not do. It does not track fingerprinting (PlayMetrics has said the data has no home there yet). It holds a staff title, not the division the person will serve, so it does not say whether a 12U license is enough for the 10U team they are about to coach (CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT; the Help Center describes license display, not age-adequacy checks). It does not enroll anyone in a field session, approve referees for the scheduling system, or take a role and division preference for team formation.

Who Does What — the Reconciliation

The rule that makes this work is simple: one owner per fact, and the Hub never contradicts the owner. Where two systems can both hold a credential during the changeover, the region's standing policy applies: the most favorable current record prevails, so a volunteer cleared in PMGS is never shown as expired because an old Affinity row says so.

Why both, and not just PMGS PMGS answers "is this person Verified?" The Hub answers "what exactly do I do next, for the division I am actually going to coach, and can you do the clicking for me?" The second question is where volunteers drop out, and it is region-specific: the class Region 58 runs after the season starts, the fingerprinting reimbursement, the referee administrator's Intermediate-grade rule for 14U. Nothing in PMGS carries that, and nothing in the Hub needs to own the official record to carry it.

  1. Sign up. A parent ticks a volunteer role while registering their player, or answers the Coaching Request; anyone else uses the Hub's Register page. Either way they end up in the Hub's candidacy with a role and divisions.
  2. The region makes them official. The registrar designates them as staff in the Club System and imports them to PMGS with the right title. This is the step that creates the AYSO membership and starts the clock on requirements.
  3. Two emails arrive. PMGS invites them to complete member requirements; the Hub (or the registrar's nudge) sends the one-time sign-in link. The Hub's My Compliance page now opens with the "Registered in PlayMetrics" banner: registration and clearances are managed in PlayMetrics, this page is for everything else.
  4. Clearances in PMGS. From their Member role: photo, waivers, Complete Screening for JDP, USSF ID for SafeSport. The Hub shows each as it lands, and keeps the SafeSport walkthrough one click away for anyone who gets lost on the Learning Center.
  5. Training through the Hub. The Hub names the course for each role and division. Online courses open through the volunteer's PlayMetrics Education tab (completions sync to PMGS and then to the Hub). Field sessions and referee courses are enrolled from the Hub's session list. Fingerprinting follows the Hub's LiveScan steps; the volunteer sends the ATI and the CVPA records it.
  6. Verified. PMGS flips them to Verified; the Hub reads it and shows "You're all set for the season"; a referee's scheduling account opens. Renewals come back the same way a year later, from both PMGS and the Hub, agreeing on the date.
What changes for a returning volunteer Nothing they will notice except fewer accounts. They stop having a Sports Connect password (the Hub never asked them to remember it anyway), they get a PlayMetrics Member role they may already have as a parent, and AYSO University opens from PlayMetrics instead of from the Hub's launch button. Their training history, licenses and grades were migrated by AYSO National; the Hub links a volunteer's older AYSOU account to their PMGS identity so a course taken under the old account still counts.

Proposed Transition Plan for Region 58

Region 18, the other pilot region on the same software, moved its official record to PMGS in September 2026 and has run the Hub against it since. Region 58 has not yet connected its Club System to the Governing System or imported its volunteers; every Region 58 volunteer is still homed on the Sports Connect bridge. The proposal is to do the switch between seasons, in the order below, so that no volunteer is mid-requirement when their source of truth moves.

Now (October 2026) — Fall season on the bridge ✅
The Hub is live on Sports Connect and Affinity. PMGS is live for pilot regions with JDP and the AYSOU connection working. The automation already knows how to read PMGS (membership status, per-requirement dates, licenses and grades) because Region 18 needed it; the Hub already knows how to present a PMGS-homed volunteer. Nothing to do for Fall volunteers; let the season finish on the system they started on.
Step 1 (November–December 2026) — Connect and import
Registrar: connect the Club System to the Governing System (Administration → More Actions → Connect to PlayMetrics Club OS, from the PMGS side), confirm the green Connected badge, then import current Fall staff with their final titles (see the compliance guide for the title tiers). Start with board, coaches and referees who are already Verified-equivalent in Affinity so the migrated data can be checked against known-good people. Automation: turn on the PMGS pulls for Region 58, link each person's PlayMetrics member number to their existing record, and publish with the most-favorable-prevails rule. Hub: no code change; imported volunteers flip to the "Registered in PlayMetrics" view automatically.
Step 2 (January 2027, before Spring registration) — PMGS-first for everyone new
New volunteers are imported to PMGS as they sign up and are never registered in Sports Connect. The Hub's Register page stops creating Sports Connect accounts for Region 58 (it already skips them for PMGS-homed people) and its delegated-registration consent text is retired. The Portal's "Not Registered" tab becomes "import to PMGS." JDP renewals are left to PMGS and the volunteer; the automation stops invoking Sterling re-checks. Fingerprinting and in-person sessions carry on exactly as now.
Step 3 (Spring 2027 season) — Prove it on a full season
Run Spring with PMGS as the only official record for Region 58 while Affinity is still readable, so any discrepancy (a license PMGS did not receive, a SafeSport that did not sync from the USSF ID) can be compared against the old export and reported to PlayMetrics or AYSO National. Decide from the evidence whether the Hub's SafeSport certificate upload is still needed. Verify the AYSOU admin path for field-session enrollment still works without Sports Connect.
Step 4 (by the Sports Connect and Affinity retirement) — Cut the bridge
Delete every stored Sports Connect credential (the authorization volunteers signed said this would happen), remove the Sports Connect sign-on launch and the Affinity scrapers, and keep only the Affinity history already captured. AYSO National relayed September 30, 2027 as the vendor's retirement date in June 2026; confirm it before planning the last step around it.
Do not do this mid-season A volunteer whose background check is pending in Affinity and whose record moves to PMGS the same week will see "in progress" become "not started" unless the migrated status arrives first. Import between seasons, import the already-cleared people first, and keep the old export readable until the new one has been checked against it for a full cycle.

Risks and Open Questions

Two AYSO University accounts for one person

PlayMetrics mints a new AYSOU learner for each member, so a veteran can end up with an old account from the Sports Connect years and a new one from PMGS. In Region 18 roughly one in twelve PMGS volunteers has both. The Hub links the two to one person so completions roll up, but merging the accounts inside AYSOU is a national admin action. Expect "I already took Safe Haven" questions during the first PMGS season and answer them from the linked record.

PMGS does not tell us the email

The membership list the automation reads carries the PlayMetrics user ID and name but no email, so the join to the Hub's identity runs through the Club System's coach-request and signup exports and, as a last resort, by name. Anyone the join cannot place is listed as unmatched rather than dropped. Region 58's overrides file is the place to pin the handful of ambiguous ones, as it was for Affinity.

Fingerprinting stays homeless

Until PMGS has a field for it, the region's own LiveScan record is the authority and the CVPA keeps reporting results to AYSO National outside PlayMetrics. The Club System's new Staff Clearance Submissions beta could hold a LiveScan proof as a custom requirement, but that would be a region-only record that AYSO National does not see — CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT, and probably not worth the second place to look.

Title tiers versus the Hub's requirement model

PMGS applies requirements by staff title; the Hub applies them by role, division and age (a minor referee is exempt from SafeSport and fingerprinting; a board member owes the Regional Board Member course). The two must agree or the volunteer sees two different lists. The proposal is that the Hub adopts PMGS's title as the base and keeps only region additions on top (fingerprinting, the division-specific course, the referee-grade rule), and that any disagreement is treated as a bug in the Hub, not in PMGS.

The "no-name region" path

AYSO National's fallback for someone with no PlayMetrics or Sports Connect record is to give them AYSOU access through a holding region and move them later by script. A person in that holding state is invisible to Region 58's automation until the move, so their training will look missing. Importing them to PMGS through our own Club System avoids the holding region entirely; the Hub's Register page exists precisely so that nobody needs it.

Privacy posture does not change

Status only for background checks, no report detail, no new copies of birth dates or addresses beyond what PMGS already holds. The Hub continues to read, never to export en masse, and the board Portal stays behind the region's Google sign-in.
