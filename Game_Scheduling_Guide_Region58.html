Game Scheduling
Sports Connect → PlayMetrics

AYSO Region 58 — Prepared for Ben Hauser  |  April 28, 2026
Bottom Line for Ben PlayMetrics has a built-in game scheduler for in-house leagues that replaces the manual scheduling process in Sports Connect. It can auto-generate weekly matchups across divisions and fields, detect coach and field conflicts, and track standings — all in one place. Field management is more powerful with drag-and-drop scheduling, sub-fields, automatic parent notifications when fields change, and calendar sync. The biggest shift: scheduling, field management, and parent communication are now interconnected instead of being separate workflows. What's Better in PlayMetrics

Auto-Generate Matchups

PM can automatically generate weekly game matchups for each division, assigning teams to fields and time slots. It tracks how often two teams have played each other and highlights teams that aren't scheduled in a given week. This replaces the manual process of building round-robin schedules in spreadsheets.

Conflict Detection

The game scheduler shows coaching conflicts across divisions (if a coach has kids in two age groups), field double-bookings, and scheduling gaps. In SC, this was a manual check — in PM, it's highlighted in the UI as you build the schedule.

Field Management with Automatic Notifications

Fields can be closed on short notice (rain, maintenance) with a few clicks. When a field is closed, PM automatically sends high-alert notifications to all affected families. No need to manually email. Field changes also update the parents' synced calendars automatically.

Practices and Games on One Calendar

In SC, practices and games were separate worlds. In PM, they share the same field calendar. This means when you're scheduling games, you can see which fields already have practices on them. Drag-and-drop interface for moving things around.

Parents See Everything in One Place

Parents see all their kids' games, practices, and events in a single consolidated calendar view. They can sync it to Google or Apple calendars. When you change a game time or close a field, their calendar updates automatically. No more "I didn't get the email."

Expected Scheduling Workflow in PlayMetrics

Based on the documentation and Superusers discussion. Specific steps will be confirmed after the scheduling webinar.

1
Set Up Locations & Fields

Before any scheduling, set up the physical fields under Locations in the PM admin. Add each field with its address, availability, sub-fields if applicable (e.g., Noble Park Field 1, Field 2, Field 3). Set time slots for when each field is available for games.

This only needs to be done once — it carries forward season to season.

2
Teams Must Be Formed First

Game scheduling can only happen after teams are built in Team Assignments and assigned to the League. The scheduler needs to know which teams exist in each division before it can generate matchups.

3
Generate or Build the Game Schedule

Two options:

  • Auto-generate: PM can create weekly matchups across divisions, assigning teams to fields and time slots automatically. It detects coach and field conflicts as it builds. View the schedule by period (week) or by day.
  • Manual: Create individual games by picking home team, away team, field, date, time, and duration. Or import games from a CSV file.

For our in-house AYSO program, auto-generate is likely the way to go — it's built specifically for this use case.

4
Review & Adjust

After generating, review the schedule for conflicts (coach double-booked, field overlap, teams not getting enough games). The scheduler highlights these visually. Make adjustments by dragging games to different slots or swapping matchups.

5
Publish & Notify

Once the schedule looks right, publish it. Parents are notified through PM and the games appear on their team calendars. If a parent has synced their calendar, it shows up on their phone automatically.

6
During the Season: Score Entry & Standings

Coaches or Team Staff enter scores after each game. Standings update automatically. If a game needs to be rescheduled or a field needs to be closed, make the change in PM and parents get notified automatically.

What We Don't Know Yet

Auto-Team Building / Balanced Teams

PlayMetrics does NOT have the same auto-team-building feature that Sports Connect had. Team assignment uses a drag-and-drop interface with player evaluation data, but there's no "serpentine auto-balance" tool. PM has acknowledged this is important for AYSO and says more information is coming. For now, teams are built manually (or imported from a spreadsheet if you build them externally).
