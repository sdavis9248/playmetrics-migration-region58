The Field & Schedule Dashboard

Operations → Schedules opens the hub. A few things worth knowing on day one:

  • Grid vs. List. Toggle between a calendar grid and a flat list. A calendar icon lets you jump to a specific day. If a field isn't shown in the list, it simply has no events scheduled on it.
  • Icon legend & color coding. Event types (practice, game, clinic, etc.) are color-coded throughout. The legend explains every color and icon.
  • Search & filter. Both are available on every schedule view — filter by facility, field, surface, etc.
  • Club Schedule shows every home/away event with attendance icons (attending / partial / injured / not attending / unsure). You can export attendance and schedules and hide cancelled events.

Fields & Facilities

This is the one-time setup that everything else hangs off. Two distinctions to get right:

Club fields Fields the region has oversight over. For our rec program, almost everything is a club field.
Travel fields Fields commonly used by teams but not managed by the region — typically Extra/competitive. Add as "travel facility."

Facilities

A facility has a name and an address. The address auto-populates a Google map that families see, and you can upload a field map image so parents know where to go inside a multi-field park. Fields are added inside the facility.

Fields & sub-fields

Each field has an identifier, a name, optional league field names, an owner (this person is notified whenever the field changes), and a surface. You can add sub-fields (e.g., a full field split into 1A / 1B for small-sided 6U/8U), each with its own ID and conflicts.

💡 How sub-field conflicts work A conflict set on a sub-field blocks its sibling sub-fields when something is scheduled — so if 1A is in use and you've flagged the conflict, 1B shows as blocked. Important caveat from the webinar: sub-field conflicts only affect the sub-fields, not the parent full field. Model this deliberately if we split fields for the little kids.

Coach Access (Read-Only)

You can turn on Coach Access so coaches can view the field schedule — see what's available or booked each day — for all teams, specific teams, or a level of play. Be clear on what this does and doesn't do:

⚠️ Coach Access is view-only Turning it on does not let coaches submit field or practice requests. Requests require either "practice plan preferences" or the newer reservation plans beta, which an admin then reviews and approves. If we want coaches to request practice slots rather than just look at the calendar, we need to ask Heather to enable that beta for Region 58.

Reservations vs. Club Events vs. Staff Events

Three different "event" buckets that are easy to confuse, and the difference is who sees them:

TypeWho sees itUse it for
ReservationsAdmin only — never shown to familiesRental, field prep, closure, coach training, field cleanup. Can be recurring. Blocks the field on the schedule.
Club EventsOutward-facing — shows on parent/player calendarsClub-wide events you want teams/families invited to. Based on team attendance.
Staff EventsSelected staff (with RSVP)Board meetings, coach meetings. Beta Off by default — must be enabled for the region. RSVP is attending / not / partial + note.

The Practice Scheduler

This is the centralized tool for practice field plans, and it's a genuine upgrade over the SC world where practices lived nowhere. First decision: do we manage practices centrally, or let coaches/team managers add their own? If coaches will self-schedule, the Practice Scheduler module (and its advanced settings) has to be enabled under Club Administration → Modules.

How it works

  • Build time slots by field and day, with start/end times. "Add time slot" stacks subsequent hours. Slots can vary by day of week.
  • Drag-and-drop teams onto slots. The right panel shows each team and how many times it's been scheduled.
  • Double-booking is allowed — you'll get a warning (e.g., "team has multiple practices that day") but it won't stop you. Useful for scrimmages or two teams sharing a field.
  • Save options: "this week only" or "through a date." The latter duplicates the week's pattern across the rest of the season — this is the trick for a team that practices the same slot every week.
  • Publish notifies all teams by default. For small edits, use "publish selected weeks" so you don't blast everyone. You can attach a note to the notification.
  • Drafts are visible to coaches; published practices appear on parent/player calendars.
  • Owner & guardrails: the scheduler owner is notified of changes. Settings let you prevent changes outside the planned time, prevent teams from cancelling, and optionally allow teams to delete practices.
💡 Practice Times color legend White = open slot  ·  Red = field closed  ·  Light blue = practice scheduled  ·  ⚠ = conflict  ·  Gray = field/slot unavailable.

Mass Alerts & Field Closures

Both live on the field schedule. This is where the biggest expectation-correction is, so read the warning.

Mass alerts (the megaphone)

Pick a message, a start/end time, and the affected fields, then choose recipients (coaches / team staff / players). PM won't let you send if there are no teams or events on the selected fields. Good for "lightning spotted, take cover 30 minutes."

🚨 Alerts are app push notifications ONLY — not text, not email Confirmed in the webinar Q&A: mass alerts go out as push notifications through the PlayMetrics app and show in the in-app notification center. They are not sent as SMS or email. For an AYSO rec population where plenty of parents won't install the app, that's a real coverage gap. Keep using our existing channels (registrar Gmail blasts / MailChimp) as the safety net for anything genuinely urgent — don't assume a PM alert reached everyone. Worth flagging to Sara so comms expectations are set.

Closing (and reopening) fields

  • Set date, start/end time, and an optional recurring pattern (handy for spring-break-style multi-day closures or a re-turf).
  • Choose whether to cancel events in progress, include team staff in notifications, and cancel affected practices immediately (with or without notifying).
  • You can instead opt to manually adjust practices and just notify the practice-schedule owners.
  • Mistakes are reversible: Operations → Schedules → More Actions → Open Fields reopens a field and can send a status-change notification. So an accidental wrong-day closure is a quick fix.

The League Game Scheduler

This is the heart of Ben's job, and PM gives only a brief overview in this webinar — the full league-operations training is in July. Here's the confirmed shape of it. It lives under Programs → Leagues.

1
Division Settings

Per division, set: division name, number of games per team, default arrival time, game duration, and the fields this division plays on. The default arrival time is your only lever for "show up early" / travel buffer — the scheduler does not calculate travel time between fields.

2
Game Periods

Set the start and end date, then toggle specific weeks off when a division isn't playing (they demoed switching off Memorial Day weekend). This is how you'd carve out, e.g., a holiday week in our fall season.

3
Select Teams & Generate Matchups

Choose the teams in the division, then the Schedule tab generates matchups. You can see a "scheduled" and a "not scheduled" view so it's obvious which teams still need games. For a recurring weekly pattern you really only build the structure once.

4
Manage Schedule (drag-and-drop grid)

This is the powerful view. A field-by-time grid across the whole league, jump from date to date, drag games between slots, and hit Check for Conflicts. Coaching conflicts surface across divisions within the league — based on the coach's team assignment, not sibling/player overlap. View Settings control the time window and increments; the Icon Legend color-codes every division; an Unassigned Games panel holds anything not yet placed.

5
Publish

Publish all changes or selected weeks, and optionally notify impacted teams with a message. Watch the unassigned-games toggle: if you leave it off, unassigned games are deleted on publish and this cannot be undone. Keep it on unless you truly mean to discard them.

✅ Public League Link — great for our website Every league (and every individual division) has a shareable public link that lets people view the schedule without a PlayMetrics account — "grandparents," as PM put it. We can post the league or division link on the Duda site so families and extended family see the schedule without logging in. Worth coordinating with Sara for the public site.

What's Better in PlayMetrics

Auto-Generate Matchups

PM generates weekly matchups per division, tracks how often two teams have played, and flags teams not scheduled in a given week — replacing manual round-robin spreadsheets.

Live Conflict Detection

Coach double-bookings (across divisions in the league) and field overlaps are highlighted in the grid as you build, instead of being a manual eyeball check at the end.

Fields, Practices & Games on One Calendar

When you schedule games you can see which fields already have practices or reservations on them. Drag-and-drop everywhere.

Parents See Everything & Can Sync It

Parents get one consolidated calendar across all their kids, can deselect teams to focus on one child, set attendance in bulk or per-event, and subscribe via iCal (full) or Google (games). When you publish a change, synced calendars update.
