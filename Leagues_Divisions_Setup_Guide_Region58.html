Leagues & Divisions Setup
Region 58 Runbook

AYSO Region 58 — Fall 2026 (Membership Year 2026) Core / Recreational  |  Prepared June 20, 2026
What This Covers A start-to-finish runbook for building the leagues and divisions that hold our Fall 2026 core/rec season in PlayMetrics. PlayMetrics organizes play as Programs → Leagues → Sanctioning Bodies. Registration (the Program) already exists; this guide stands up the operational In-House league and its divisions on top of it. Travel leagues and sanctioning bodies are for EXTRA/Alliance competitive play and are not needed here. Work through the pre-flight checklist first, then the phases in order. Every decision point is flagged with a recommended default, and every step whose exact in-product label we haven't yet confirmed is marked so it can be verified live or with PlayMetrics support.
A note on confidence The PlayMetrics league-creation flow below is confirmed from the Competitive League Operations webinar. The deeper recreational mechanics — the exact path to create a division, set roster size, and choose draft vs. director — were deferred by PlayMetrics to a separate "recreational league operations" training that hasn't happened yet. Those steps are written to the structure we know is correct, but the precise button labels should be confirmed in-product or with success@playmetrics.com. Each is marked CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT.

Pre-Flight Checklist

Confirm all of the following before creating the league. The league pulls its season, classification, teams, and tagged documents from things that must already exist upstream.

  • Registration program is live. The Fall 2026 Core registration program exists and is open/collecting (ours is). The league will draw players and divisions from this program's season.
  • The Fall 2026 / MY2026 season is defined in PlayMetrics with its playing dates, classification, and sport set. League creation auto-populates from this — if it's wrong here, it's wrong everywhere downstream.
  • Facilities / fields are entered. Not strictly required to create divisions, but required later for game scheduling, so enter them now to avoid "field not found" rework.
  • Resource tags exist. Every region ships with birth certificate and player photo tags by default (Club Administration → Edit Settings → Player Resource Tags). Only relevant if we choose to verify documents (Phase 5) — no action needed otherwise.
  • Admin access confirmed. Both Steven and Sara have admin access to the club system, and you've agreed who will be the league owner (Phase 1).
  • Division plan agreed. The Region 58 division list, roster sizes, and formation methods (table at the end) are approved by Sara and the Division Coordinators.

Phase 1 Create the Fall 2026 In-House League

Navigation: Leagues → Create League (build from scratch — do not copy a Travel/EXTRA league).

  1. League Name. PlayMetrics recommends being explicit with verbiage. Use our season-naming convention so it reads unambiguously in lists and exports.
    Decision — League name Recommended default: 2026 Fall Core (matches the naming we already use in registration and data exports, e.g. "2025 Fall Core"). Keep it consistent so the league, season, and CSV exports all line up.
  2. Owner. Assign a league owner.
    Decision — Owner Recommended default: Steven (Registrar) as owner, with Sara retaining admin access regionally. Whether PlayMetrics allows more than one owner per league is CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT — if co-ownership is supported, add Sara as a second owner; if not, ownership can be reassigned later.
  3. League Type → In-House. Select In-House. This is the rec/core container: it unlocks team assignments, divisions, and generated game schedules. After creation, an In-House league shows more tabs than a Travel league.
    Decision — League type (effectively fixed) For core/rec, the answer is always In-House. Travel is only for EXTRA/Alliance teams that import schedules from outside competitions and need roster-movement guardrails — not applicable to this season. Do not select Travel.
  4. Season. Select the Fall 2026 / MY2026 core season. PlayMetrics auto-populates classification, playing season, and sport from it. Verify those auto-filled values look right before continuing.
  5. Sanctioning Body. Leave unlinked for core. (See Phase 5 — sanctioning bodies are an optional player-document ledger, not needed for rec.)
  6. Teams. Select teams from the season (covered in Phase 4). You can refine team selection after creation, so it's fine to proceed and add/adjust later.
  7. Create League. Click Create League. You'll land in the league with its Teams / Players / Divisions / Games tabs available.

Phase 2 Set Up the Divisions

Divisions live inside the In-House league (league → divisions → teams). PlayMetrics' Division is the equivalent of the old SportsConnect "Division" — the age/gender flight that holds teams. We are creating 16 team divisions: eight age groups, each Boys and Girls.

Decision — Division naming convention Recommended default: keep our zero-padded, gendered convention — "06U Boys", "06U Girls", "10U Boys", … "19U Girls". Zero-padding (06U, 07U, 08U before 10U) is what makes divisions sort youngest-to-oldest in PlayMetrics lists; without it, "10U" sorts before "6U". Apply it to every division.

Per-division settings

For each of the 16 divisions, set its roster size (max players per team) and its formation method (draft vs. director-formed). Use the values in the checklist table at the end of this guide.

Confirm in-product — division create path & roster/draft labels The exact path to add a division, the field that sets roster size / max roster, and the toggle that distinguishes a draft division from a director-formed one were not shown in the webinar (rec operations were deferred). The likely locations are within the league's Divisions tab and PlayMetrics' team-formation / draft tooling, but confirm the labels live or with success@playmetrics.com before relying on them. Do not assume a label that isn't on screen.
Decision — Formation method per division Recommended default follows our existing practice: 6U, 7U, 8U are director-formed (no draft — coordinators place players directly). 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, 19U run a DRAFT. For the draft divisions, also decide when each draft runs and lock team rosters only after the draft completes — schedule those draft events on the regional calendar so coordinators aren't drafting against a moving registration count.

Order of operations within a division

1. Create the division using the naming convention.   2. Set its roster size.   3. Set its formation method (director vs. draft).   4. Leave team creation/seating until registration numbers are firm (Phase 4). Director divisions can be seated as players come in; draft divisions wait for the draft.

Phase 3 Non-Team Programs — 5U Schoolyard & KickStart

These are handled differently. They have no teams, no rosters, and no draft, so they are not built as competitive divisions with team capacity. Their capacity is governed by a registration cap on the program itself, and coaches do not drive registration demand.

05U Schoolyard Non-team

Fun-soccer learning sessions in groups of roughly 20. No teams, no rosters, no draft. Manage headcount through the program's registration capacity, not through team slots. If a division object is needed at all for reporting, name it 05U (zero-padded) so it sorts at the top — but it carries no roster size or formation method.

KickStart (if offered) Jamboree

A jamboree program with an entered registration cap. No teams are formed; coach signups do not affect the registration count. Manage purely by the cap.

Confirm in-product — do these need a league at all? Because Schoolyard and KickStart have no teams or schedules, they most likely live entirely at the Program / registration level (capacity set by registration cap) and do not require an In-House league or divisions. Confirm with PlayMetrics whether any league object is needed for them, or whether the registration cap alone is sufficient. The field/label where the registration cap is set should be confirmed in-product.

Phase 4 Teams & Capacity

Teams are pulled from the season selected at league creation and grouped under their divisions.

  • Roster size = the per-team cap for that division (the values in the checklist table). It defines how many players a team can hold.
  • Target number of teams ≈ projected registrations ÷ roster size, rounded to keep rosters near full without overloading. Fill the target-teams column once registration counts are firm.
  • Director divisions (6U–8U): seat players into teams directly as they register, keeping each team at or under its roster size.
  • Draft divisions (10U–19U): hold team seating until the draft; the draft assigns players into the team count you've set for that division.
Confirm in-product — where team capacity is enforced Whether the roster size is set at the division level (applies to all teams) or per individual team, and the exact field label, is CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT. Set it wherever PlayMetrics exposes "max roster" / "team capacity" for In-House divisions.

Phase 5 Optional — Document Verification & Sanctioning Body

This entire phase is optional and, for a core/rec season, recommended off.

Decision — Verify birth certificate / player photo? Recommended default: No for core/rec. AYSO does not require birth-certificate verification for any play type or competition level — it is purely a regional choice, and turning it on adds per-player admin load. If the board wants age/photo verification anyway, it's done in the club system (below), not the Governing System.
Decision — Create a sanctioning body? Recommended default: No for core/rec. A sanctioning body is a ledger for tracking player documents required by an outside league (e.g., a state association an EXTRA team registers with). Core/rec has no external registration requirement, so skip it.

If — and only if — the region decides to verify documents, the sequence is:

  1. Confirm the program's document questions are tagged (birth certificate → "birth certificate", player photo → "player photo") so files flow downstream into the league. Edit Program → Questions → Advanced Options.
  2. Create a sanctioning body and add verification requirements (resource tag, effective date, expiry — e.g., photo valid 2 years, birth certificate "do not expire"). Club Administration → Leagues / Sanctioning Bodies — CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT for the exact path.
  3. Link that sanctioning body to the league (Phase 1, step 5).
Do not touch Clearance Tracking The "Clearance Tracking" toggle at the bottom of the sanctioning-body screen is for volunteer/coach clearances (background checks, coaching courses). Leave it alone. Those are tracked in the AYSO Governing System, not here, with only a rolled-up cleared/not-cleared status flowing back into the club system.

Final Verification Before opening play

Walk this list before the season is treated as live. The goal is to catch a wrong roster size or a mis-sorted division now, not after teams are seated.

  • All 16 team divisions exist under the 2026 Fall Core In-House league — every age group has both a Boys and a Girls division.
  • Names sort youngest-to-oldest. The list reads 05U, 06U, 07U, 08U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, 19U — proving the zero-padding is applied. Any "10U" appearing before "06U" means a division is missing its leading zero.
  • Roster sizes match the table exactly: 6 / 7 / 7 / 9 / 12 / 14 / 14 / 22 across 6U→19U.
  • Formation method is correct per division: 6U–8U director-formed, 10U–19U draft. Draft events are on the regional calendar.
  • Team counts are sane: for each division, target teams ≈ registrations ÷ roster size, with no team over its roster cap.
  • Non-team programs carry a registration cap and have no teams, no draft, no roster — Schoolyard and KickStart only.
  • Verification decision is applied: either no sanctioning body / no document verification (recommended), or — if the board chose to verify — the sanctioning body is linked and Clearance Tracking is left untouched.
  • Cross-check against registration. The league's divisions line up one-to-one with the age/gender groups in the Fall 2026 registration program — no orphans on either side.
  • Sara has reviewed the division list and team targets with the Division Coordinators.
When all boxes are ticked The league and divisions are ready. Facilities and game scheduling come next (the upcoming PlayMetrics "recreational league operations" session covers team assignment and schedule generation in depth) — but the structural backbone for Fall 2026 core play is in place.
