Volunteer Compliance
Implementation Guide

AYSO Region 58 — Fall 2026 Season  |  Updated October 25, 2026
← All Region 58 Guides
Bottom Line Sports Connect and the Sports Affinity association platform are being retired, and with them the old way volunteers reached AYSOU and the old way the region tracked clearances. Volunteer compliance now runs entirely through PlayMetrics. The Club System (blue interface) captures volunteer interest and designates staff; the Governing System (purple interface, operated by AYSO National) is where each volunteer completes requirements — JDP background screening, SafeSport through the U.S. Soccer Learning Center, and AYSOU courses through eTrainU — and where the region tracks who is Verified. The Governing System is live and the AYSOU connection works: past course completions were synced in, and new completions arrive automatically. Fingerprinting (California LiveScan) is the one requirement that still lives outside PlayMetrics.

What Changed With the Shutdown

This guide was first written in April 2026, when the Governing System was not yet open to pilot regions and returning volunteers still reached AYSOU by logging in to Sports Connect and clicking through to the Sports Affinity site. Both of those facts are now history. For a region, the shutdown changes these things:

⚠️ The Sports Affinity Workaround Is Gone The April version of this guide said Region 58's Sports Affinity / eTrainU automation would keep covering compliance tracking during the transition, and that new volunteers without a Sports Connect account could be added directly in Sports Affinity to get an AYSO ID. Neither path exists anymore. Every volunteer, returning or new, goes through the Club System → Governing System pipeline described below. If a volunteer tells you they "did it in Sports Connect," they still need to be imported into the Governing System so their record and their legacy data connect.

A volunteer cannot be added to the Governing System for background screening and certifications unless they first exist as a staff member in the Club System. Being added to the Governing System is what triggers compliance: the email, the requirements list, the JDP link, and the AYSOU connection. PlayMetrics' own metaphor is worth keeping in mind — the Club System is the registration desk, the Governing System is the compliance office.

Step 2 — Designate Every Volunteer as Staff Club System

Every volunteer must be categorized as staff in the Club System before they can be imported into the Governing System. PlayMetrics is explicit that this is a functional requirement, not a label: it grants the volunteer their permissions and it is the bridge the import reads from.

Volunteer Type Staff Designation How They Get There
Regional Commissioner, Treasurer, Registrar, CVPA, other admins Admin Staff → Admins. Eligible for the Governing System import as soon as they are added.
Division Coordinators Director Staff → Directors. Eligible for import as soon as they are added.
Head Coaches, Assistant Coaches Coach Option 1: assign directly to a team through League → Assignments. Option 2: bulk import without a team through Staff → Coaches → More Actions → Import Coaches, so compliance can start before teams are formed.
Referees, Board Members, Team Parents, Concessions, Field Prep, etc. Team Staff Assigned to hidden "Volunteer" container teams. Requires a team import plus a Team Staff import (below).

Non-coach volunteers: the hidden container-team workaround

PlayMetrics still requires Team Staff to be attached to a team, and it still describes the container-team approach as a temporary plan until system updates are finalized. Until League Volunteer Signups replaces it, this is the documented path.

  1. Prepare settings. Add a Volunteer level under Club Administration → Edit Settings → Add Level, allow Co-Ed Teams, and set Team Staff titles under Staff → Team Staff → More Actions → Manage Titles to the standardized AYSO list below.
  2. Import container teams. Use the AYSO Import Templates and Teams → More Actions → Import Teams. One team per role (Referee Team, Board Member Team, …), gender Co-Ed, level Volunteer, season = the current season name.
  3. Hide them. Teams → More Actions → Set Visibility, filter by label Volunteer, select all, uncheck every role. The teams stay functional but invisible to families.
  4. Export volunteers. From the program: Programs → [Program] → More Actions → Export Volunteers (and Export Responses if you need the question answers). From signups: Volunteer Signups → [Signup] → More Actions → Export Volunteers. Leave head and assistant coaches out — they go through the Coach path.
  5. Import as Team Staff. Map name, email, phone, title, season and team name into the Team Staff import file, then Staff → Team Staff → More Actions → Import Team Staff. Duplicates are skipped, so you can run it again as volunteers trickle in. Single late additions can be added by hand on the team.

Step 3 — Add Volunteers to the Governing System Governing System

Switch to the Governing System by clicking your profile name in the upper-left corner and choosing your Club Admin role (the navigation turns purple). Anyone who manages regional compliance can be added as an additional Club Admin under Administration → Admins. Your Club System must show as Connected under Administration → More Actions → Connect to PlayMetrics Club OS; a system admin for the club does that once.

The import, step by step

Membership → Staff → More Actions → Import Staff → Import from Club OS. For each Staff Title enabled in the AYSO governing instance, click +, use the selection worksheet and filters to pick the volunteers who belong under that title, and Save Selection. Repeat for the next title. The Import Summary shows how many will be assigned, updated or skipped, and lists errors (download it if anything is skipped). Click Import Staff. Finalizing the import sends each volunteer the email that starts their compliance.

✅ You Control Who Gets Sent — and Therefore What It Costs Only the volunteers you import are asked to screen. Interested-but-unconfirmed parents never trigger a JDP charge. Import coaches once Division Coordinators have confirmed them, and import referees and board members as they commit.

Step 4 — What the Volunteer Does Governing System, Member role

Most volunteers complete this on their phone. Nothing in it requires the region to chase an email: once imported, the volunteer can log in at any time and pick up where they left off.

  1. The email. "You've been added to Region 58 for AYSO. You have requirements to complete," with a login link. An existing PlayMetrics user (a coach or parent) simply gains a new Member role for AYSO in the role switcher under their profile name. A brand-new user creates a PlayMetrics account first.
  2. Open the requirements. In the Member role, Membership → Complete Requirements. The list shows everything the volunteer's title requires.
  3. Member data, photo, waivers. Gender, date of birth, address; a clear forward-facing headshot (it prints on the member card); and the digital waivers such as the AYSO Expected Conduct form.
  4. SafeSport (Tier 1). Under Clearances, enter the USSF ID or the email used in the U.S. Soccer Learning Center, or follow the purple link to create a Learning Center account. The completion and expiration sync back automatically. Only SafeSport is needed — no other USSF course. Where the organization allows it, a certificate can be uploaded for manual verification instead.
  5. Background screening. Click Complete Screening; it hands off to JDP. "Submitted" appears about a minute after JDP receives the request, and the purple button disappears once the screening clears. If the button is greyed out, the other requirements have to be finished first. Volunteers whose Sports Connect screening was valid for the current season see it already satisfied.
  6. AYSOU courses. Under Education. A volunteer with no AYSOU account clicks Create Account next to the requirement, then Complete Course to finish setting up eTrainU and open the course. Past completions were synced from AYSOU into PlayMetrics, and new completions now flow in automatically. The PlayMetrics-to-AYSOU connection persists from one membership year to the next.
  7. Submit. The member's own status shows under Membership.

💡 "Why Can't I See My Courses?" The most common support question. A volunteer who is also an admin or coach has several roles. AYSOU and the requirements list live under the Member role, not the Admin role — they switch roles by clicking their profile name in the upper-left corner. If they already have an AYSOU account but PlayMetrics tells them to create a new one, or they discover two AYSOU accounts, that is an AYSO National fix: support@ayso.org.

Reviewing

Tick one or more volunteers in Pending Member or In Club Review and click View Requirements. From there you can read the submitted data and documents, edit details, upload a document on the volunteer's behalf, send a Request Updates message naming exactly what is missing, or Verify. If anything still needs National approval, Verify moves the volunteer to In Governing Review rather than Verified.

Expirations and renewals

The Expirations tab under Membership → Staff is the renewal desk. About 45 days before a requirement lapses the volunteer shows as Notifying Soon; at 30 days PlayMetrics emails them and opens the requirement for resubmission (they cannot renew earlier than that); reminders repeat weekly until the date; and on expiry they are notified and drop out of Verified until they resubmit. Mark a volunteer Not Renewed when they are not continuing, so the reminders stop. Background checks remain annual.

✅ This Is the Systemic Gate Jay Asked For A single Verified / not-Verified state per volunteer, maintained by the system, visible to the region, and enforced on official rosters and member cards. The manual spreadsheet chase that defined Sports Affinity season prep is replaced by reading one tab. Fingerprinting is the exception — see below.

Requirement by Requirement

Background check (JDP)

Started from PlayMetrics, performed by JDP, valid for one year. The region decides who is sent; AYSO National's compliance team decides outcomes, and a volunteer is ultimately marked ineligible at the National level, not by the region. A volunteer who holds two roles is screened once — JDP flags the duplicate. Volunteers whose previous result was rainbow-adjudicated (yellow, orange, brown) were imported without a status and go through JDP fresh. Youth under 18 are gatekept from screening and treated as satisfied until their 18th birthday, when the system notifies them that screening is now required.

SafeSport

Taken in the U.S. Soccer Learning Center, never in AYSOU. The volunteer links their USSF ID in PlayMetrics and the status stays current on its own. Required for Tier 1; youth are exempt. 19U players who turn 18 during the season also need SafeSport: in the Governing System, Membership → Players → 18+ Youth Players lists them (Upcoming at roughly 45 days out, Missing Accounts once 18). Set the player's own email and send the registration invitation so they get a Member role and can verify their SafeSport.

AYSOU courses (Safe Haven, role training, coaching licenses, referee grades)

Hosted by eTrainU, reached through the Education tab in the Member role. Past completions were synced into PlayMetrics and new ones arrive automatically; coaching licenses and referee grades now appear on the member's governing profile. If a course is complete in AYSOU but missing in PlayMetrics, that is a sync question for success@playmetrics.com; anything about the AYSOU account itself goes to support@ayso.org. Having eTrainU admin access for the RC, CVPA, Registrar and Division Coordinators is still useful for looking up a training report when a volunteer insists they finished a course.

Concussion Awareness — now state-specific and now expiring

AYSO rebuilt the concussion requirement to meet each state's law. California volunteers take Concussion Awareness (AZ, CA, CT, FL, GA, IL, KS, MT, NE, NM). Expiration was never enforced before; it is now, on the state's schedule. A long-time volunteer whose concussion certificate suddenly reads Expired is seeing expected behavior, not a data error — they retake the course. Questions go to support@ayso.org.

Fingerprinting (California LiveScan, AB 506)

The one requirement PlayMetrics does not hold. California law requires fingerprinting before a volunteer is activated on a team, so Region 58 continues to run the fingerprint fair, track LiveScan results, and report them to AYSO National. PlayMetrics' published position is that a LiveScan migration process will be defined once the functionality to hold that data is built. Until then a coach needs two green lights: Verified in the Governing System and fingerprints cleared on the region's list. (AYSO Wiki: California AB 506)

Youth volunteers (under 18)

Import under the Player Support title (Tier 3): youth referees, youth coach assistants, Epic Buddies. No background check and no SafeSport until 18; role training still applies. The exemption ends on the 18th birthday, and PlayMetrics prompts them for screening then.

Region 58 Season Rules

Jay (CVPA) set two enforcement gates for Fall 2026 after the AYSO Expo discussions, and they carry forward as standing policy. The goal is unchanged: stop chasing coaches who already have a team and a schedule.

Gate 1: No team without a cleared background check and fingerprints

Enforced at team formation, before Division Coordinators assign coaches and before Ben starts building schedules. In PlayMetrics terms: a coach is assigned to a team only when they show Verified (or at minimum a cleared screening) in the Governing System and appear on the region's cleared LiveScan list. Coaches are notified the moment they are assigned to a team, so clear them first.

Gate 2: No game card without the video training by Game 1

Safe Haven, SafeSport, Concussion Awareness and the age-appropriate coaching course have a hard stop at the first game. The Governing System's Verified status is the check; an unverified coach does not print on the official roster.

Fingerprint fair — early August, every year

Early enough that results are back before practices start, and paired with coach meetings or coach training so coaches make one trip. Put it on the calendar when registration opens and over-communicate it.

💡 The Yearly Rhythm Registration closes (July) → import confirmed coaches into the Governing System and hold the fingerprint fair (early August) → Verified + LiveScan cleared → teams formed → Ben builds the schedule (August to early September) → first games. Renewals happen in the Expirations tab through the year; the heavy lift is the summer import.

Key Details to Know

Personal emails for compliance

Every volunteer uses a personal email in PlayMetrics, not a role address such as rc@ayso58.org. The email is how PlayMetrics matches a person to their AYSOU record and carries the connection across membership years; a role address hands next year's RC the wrong compliance profile.

Coaches are notified immediately; players are not

Assigning a coach to a team notifies the coach at once. Players and families hear nothing until you click Notify. Clear a coach before you assign them.

One volunteer, many teams

A volunteer can sit on as many teams as needed — a referee or board member can be on a container team and a real team at the same time — and is screened once.

Work independently of the volunteer

Import, assign to teams and invite in parallel. You do not wait for a volunteer to accept anything before organizing them, and you can see at a glance who has not acted and send reminders from the system.

Birth certificates are a player matter, not a volunteer one

Player document verification (birth certificates, photos) runs through the league's sanctioning body in the Club System and is covered in the Team & Player Roster Setup Guide, Phase 9. It does not touch the Governing System volunteer pipeline described here.
