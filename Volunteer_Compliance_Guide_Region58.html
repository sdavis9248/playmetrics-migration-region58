Volunteer Compliance
Implementation Guide

AYSO Region 58 — Fall 2026 Season  |  Updated April 27, 2026
Bottom Line Volunteer compliance in PlayMetrics works through a two-system model: the Club System (our registration platform — the blue interface) captures volunteer interest and designates staff roles, and the Governing System (AYSO National — the purple interface) handles background checks and certifications. Volunteers must flow from Club → Governing to trigger compliance. Right now, we can do everything on the Club side. The Governing side is not yet accessible to pilot regions — PlayMetrics is finalizing integrations with JDP (background checks) and AYSOU (certifications), expected this summer. In the meantime, returning volunteers continue using AYSOU through Sports Connect. AYSO National (Paula) has decided to pre-import existing risk data and certifications into the PM Governing System backend ahead of AYSOU availability — this data surfaces when a volunteer is added to the Governing System.

A volunteer cannot be imported into the AYSO Governing System for background checks and certifications unless they first exist as a staff member in the Club System. This is the critical pipeline — every volunteer must go through these steps in order.

Region 58 Enforcement Plan (Jay's Proposal)

Jay (CVPA) laid out a two-step compliance enforcement plan for Fall 2026, following discussion at AYSO Expo. The core goal: stop chasing coaches who already have teams and a schedule to comply. Get ahead of it.

Jay's Nightmare Scenario

"Someone who has not done background check or fingerprint gets a team and starts practicing in August, and then a yellow or red flag comes up in their background." This is what we're preventing.

Step 1: No Team Without Background Check + Fingerprinting

Any coach who has not completed their background check and fingerprinting does not get a team. Period. This is enforced at the team formation level, before Ben starts building schedules. California AB 506 requires fingerprinting to "activate teams and print official rosters" — this isn't just AYSO policy, it's state law. (AYSO Wiki: California AB 506)

Step 2: No Game Card Without Video Training by Game 1

Coaches get a little more time for the video training courses (SafeSport, Safe Haven, Concussion Awareness, etc.), but the cutoff is Game 1. Either the training is complete or they don't get a schedule/game card. No more begging and chasing mid-season.

R58 Fingerprint Fair — Early August

Jay wants to organize a Region 58 fingerprint fair in early August (not the end of the month) so there's enough time to verify results before teams practice. Sara suggested coordinating fingerprinting with coach meetings or coach training so coaches make one trip to the park for multiple things. This needs to be on the calendar early and over-communicated to families.

✅ PlayMetrics Is Building Exactly What Jay Wants The PM Governing System's end goal is a "verified for the season" status that combines risk screening + SafeSport + coaching license into a single green/red indicator. When a coach is assigned to a team, the system checks compliance. This is the systemic enforcement Jay has been asking for — no more manual tracking and chasing. We won't have full age-specific coaching license verification in Phase 1, but the risk screening + SafeSport foundation will be there. In the meantime, our existing Sports Affinity/eTrainU automation handles the tracking.
💡 How This Connects to Scheduling Jay's compliance gates feed directly into Ben's scheduling timeline. Ben needs finalized coach lists from DCs to start building schedules. If compliance must be cleared before team assignment, then the fingerprint fair and BG check deadlines need to happen before team formation — which needs to happen before Ben starts scheduling. The rough sequence: Registration closes (July) → Fingerprint fair (early Aug) → Compliance verified → Teams formed → Ben builds schedule (Aug–early Sep) → First games (Sep 19).

Staff Designation: Who Goes Where

Every volunteer must be categorized as "staff" in the Club System before they can flow to the Governing System. Different volunteer types have different paths:

Volunteer Type Staff Designation How They Get There
Regional Commissioner, Treasurer, other admins Admin Added under Staff → Admins. Automatically syncs to Governing once added.
Division Coordinators Director Added under Staff → Directors. Automatically syncs to Governing once added.
Head Coaches, Assistant Coaches Coach Option 1: Assign directly to a team via League → Assignments (coach icon appears next to player name).
Option 2: Bulk import via Staff → Coaches → More Actions → Import Coaches.
Referees, Board Members, Team Parents, Concessions, Field Prep, etc. Team Staff Assigned to hidden "Volunteer" container teams. Requires team import + team staff import (see below).
💡 Why Hidden Container Teams? The system currently requires all Team Staff to be associated with a team. Since non-coaching volunteers aren't on player teams, we create invisible "container" teams (one per role: Referee Team, Board Member Team, etc.) just to house them administratively. These teams are hidden from public view — they're only for compliance tracking.

Non-Coach Volunteer Setup Steps Do After Go-Live

This is the most complex piece and doesn't need to happen before registration opens. Do this once you have volunteers who've expressed interest and you're ready to start compliance.

Step 1: Club Settings Prep

  • Add a "Volunteer" level under Club Administration → Edit Settings → Add Level
  • Enable Co-Ed Teams in Club Settings
  • Update Team Staff Titles under Staff → Team Staff → More Actions → Manage Titles

Step 2: Import Volunteer Team Shells

  • Download the AYSO Pilot Import Template
  • Import teams via Teams → More Actions → Import Teams
  • Each team = one volunteer role (Referee Team, Board Member Team, etc.)
  • Set gender to Co-Ed, level to Volunteer, season to your Fall 2026 season name

Step 3: Hide Volunteer Teams

  • Teams → More Actions → Set Visibility
  • Filter by Label "Volunteer" → Select All → Uncheck all roles
  • This keeps them functional but invisible to parents and coaches

Step 4: Export Volunteer Data

  • From Program: Programs → [Fall 2026 Core] → More Actions → Export Volunteers
  • From Volunteer Sign Up: Volunteer Signups → [Your Signup] → More Actions → Export Volunteers
  • Exclude head coaches and assistant coaches (they go through the Coach pathway, not Team Staff)

Step 5: Import as Team Staff

  • Map exported data into the Team Staff Import template (name, email, phone, title, season, team name)
  • Staff → Team Staff → More Actions → Import Team Staff
  • Duplicates are handled automatically — same person won't be imported twice
  • You can do multiple import rounds as new volunteers trickle in

Compliance Tiers (From AYSO National)

Paula (AYSO National) is defining tiered compliance requirements that map to Governing System roles. These determine what each volunteer must complete. The tiers are still being finalized, but the current thinking:

Tier Requirements Who It's For
Tier 1 — Full Clearance Background check + SafeSport + job-specific training Coaches 10U+, referees (10U+), anyone with unsupervised player contact
Tier 2 — Reduced Background check + job-specific training (no SafeSport) 6U/8U coaches and refs where parents are on the field (not unsupervised contact). Discussion ongoing — some regions may require Tier 1 for all coaches regardless.
Tier 3 — Youth No background check — exempt until 18th birthday Youth referees, youth coach assistants. JDP will warn if someone under 18 attempts screening, and PM will gatekeep at entry.
⚠️ Tiers Are Still Being Finalized Paula is working through edge cases with the Superuser group (e.g., Kickstart/Epic Buddy leaders as "Player Support" vs coaches, whether regions can mandate Tier 1 for all). The tier a volunteer is assigned in the Governing System determines their compliance requirements — regions control which tier each volunteer gets mapped to.

Import Workflow: What Actually Happens

Pierce clarified the exact mechanics of importing volunteers during the Superusers call. This is important because it means we can get ahead of things without chasing people:

Step 1: Import

We import volunteers (from SC data or manually). They sit in the system in the background. At this point, we choose whether or not to invite them right away.

Step 2: Organize

We can assign coaches to teams and organize staff BEFORE they verify their account. We don't need to wait for them to act first.

Step 3: Invite

When we're ready, we send invitations. Volunteers get prompted to verify their account (first name, last name, email, phone). That's it — they can't do anything else in PM yet (no background screening available), which is fine for now.

Step 4: Track & Remind

We can see who has verified and who hasn't. Send reminders through the system to anyone who hasn't finished. Custom reminder messages supported.

✅ Key Takeaway: We Work Independently of the Volunteers We don't need to chase volunteers before we can organize. Import → assign to teams → invite in parallel. They catch up on their end when they're ready. This is a much better workflow than SC where you had to wait for account acceptance.

Legacy Data Migration (AYSO National Plan)

Paula has decided to pre-import existing risk data and certifications from the SC Association site into the PM Governing System before AYSOU is available. Here's how this works:

Two-Batch Approach

  • Batch 1 (soon): All existing volunteer data — background check status, certifications — imported so regions can start the compliance clearance process. Spreads the work out rather than handing the whole pool on July 1st.
  • Batch 2 (before JDP/AYSOU go live): Incremental update capturing any courses completed or background checks processed in the intervening months. Gets everyone up to date before the switches flip.

How Legacy Data Connects

  • Imported data sits on the PM Governing System backend — it's not visible until the volunteer is actually added to the Governing System
  • When a volunteer profile is created in the Governing System, PM does a lookup, recognizes the legacy data, and satisfies the matching requirements automatically
  • Existing background checks honored until their expiration date (annual renewal)
  • Green status (cleared) → maps to "Cleared" in PM
  • Red status (do not use / disqualified) → maps to "Not Cleared"
  • Rainbow-adjudicated (yellow/brown/orange) → imported with no background check status — they'll go through the process fresh
  • Blue (youth) → exempt, considered satisfied until 18th birthday

Governing System (AYSO National) Not Yet Accessible

Once volunteers are designated as staff in the Club System, the next step is importing them into the Governing System (the purple interface) to trigger compliance. However:

⛔ Governing System Access Is Not Available Yet PlayMetrics confirmed during the Volunteer Compliance webinar (April 2026): "Right now you don't have access to it… If we give you access to it now, it's not helpful because you might accidentally send volunteers across and the full compliance process is not set up as we need it to." Access will be granted once JDP and AYSOU integrations are finalized.

What It Will Look Like (When Available)

Based on the webinar demo:

  • You'll have a Club Admin role in the Governing System, accessible by toggling between accounts (blue = Club, purple = Governing)
  • Under Membership → Staff, you'll click More Actions → Import Staff → Import from Club OS
  • This opens a selection worksheet showing all staff in your Club System — you select who to send for compliance
  • You choose which Governing System role each volunteer maps to (Coach, Referee, Board Member, etc.) — this determines their compliance requirements
  • Once imported, volunteers get an automatic email with a link to complete their compliance requirements
  • The region controls who gets sent — this saves money because not every "interested" volunteer goes through a background screen, only confirmed ones
  • Compliance status flows back to the Club System via a "Connected Systems" area so you can track it without staying in the purple interface

What the Volunteer Sees

  • They receive an email: "You've been added to Region 58 for AYSO. You have requirements to complete."
  • They log into PlayMetrics → see their member role in the Governing System
  • Click "Complete Requirements" → provide info (gender, DOB, address), agree to waivers, upload photo
  • Click "Complete Screening" → redirected to JDP for background check
  • SafeSport and certifications will be pulled from AYSOU (integration pending)
  • Fully mobile-friendly — most volunteers complete it on their phones
  • No need to chase emails — once added to Governing, they can log in anytime and complete requirements

Key Details to Know

Personal Emails for Compliance

Everyone should use their personal email for PlayMetrics compliance, not region-role emails (like rc@ayso58.org). This is the data connectivity point for matching PM accounts to AYSOU records. Annika emphasized this repeatedly during the Superusers call — using role emails means next year's RC inherits the wrong compliance profile.

Coaches Get Notified Immediately

When you assign a coach to a team, they're notified immediately. Players are only notified when you click "Notify" — different trigger timing.

One Volunteer, Multiple Teams

A single volunteer can be assigned to as many teams as needed. No limitation of one team per person. Useful for referees and board members who serve across the whole region.

Compliance Requirements Vary by Role

When importing to the Governing System, the role you assign determines compliance requirements. See the Compliance Tiers section above — Tier 1 (full clearance) vs Tier 2 (reduced) vs Tier 3 (youth exempt). The region controls who gets sent and at which tier, which controls cost.

Account Ownership Is Player-Centric

Unlike SC's parent-centric model, PlayMetrics is player-centric. Account owners control which players are shared with which contacts. You can transfer ownership via admin, but the target must have a verified account first. If flipping account owner with multiple kids, you must switch on each player separately. Krista (Region 18) has been cleaning up account ownership during her import — much easier to fix in PM than in SC.

eTrainU Admin Access Recommended

Paula is recommending that all coach admins (RC, CVPA, registrar, division coordinators) have admin access in eTrainU during the interim period. This lets you look up training reports directly if someone swears they have certifications that didn't show up in the import.

Birth Certificate Verification — Separate from Volunteers

Existing BC verifications from Sports Connect transfer through an embedded lookup process. You can set PlayMetrics to delete the BC file after verification. Ongoing verification responsibility stays with the region.
