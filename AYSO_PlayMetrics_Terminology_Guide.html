AYSO ↔ PlayMetrics
Terminology Guide

For AYSO volunteers navigating the SportsConnect → PlayMetrics transition  |  Updated October 26, 2026
What This Is PlayMetrics was built for competitive travel clubs, not recreational organizations like AYSO. Many concepts are the same — but the names are different, and the organizational hierarchy is structured differently. This guide maps AYSO and SportsConnect terminology against PlayMetrics equivalents, with notes on where the concepts don't map cleanly. This is a living document — contributions welcome.

System-Level Concepts

AYSO / SportsConnectPlayMetricsNotes
SportsConnect (BlueSombrero) Club System The registration and operations platform your region uses day-to-day. Sometimes called "Club OS." The interface is blue.
Portal Instance SC called your region's copy of the system a "portal." PlayMetrics calls it your instance (or Club System). AYSO Glossary
Play Level Competition Level / Level Levels (Core, EXTRA, Alliance, Kickstart, Epic, Adult) are set once under organization settings and every team is attached to one. The Governing System matches teams to AYSO by competition level when it syncs them. AYSO Glossary
Sports Affinity Governing System The national-level system for background checks, certifications, and member IDs. In PM, this is a separate interface you toggle into — same login, different account. The interface is purple. It also generates the official AYSO rosters and member cards. Adding Staff via the Connected Club System
AYSO National Governing Body The organization above all regions. Operates the Governing System, sets the compliance requirements, and migrated the legacy Sports Affinity data. Its registration requirements do not arrive automatically: the region creates the National Player Fee as a Club Fee and adds the AYSO National player questions and waivers (Code of Conduct, Liability Release) to its own program from the AYSO wiki. MY2026 Launch Checklist
Region Club Your AYSO region = one PM club instance. Introduction to the Club System
Area / Section No direct equivalent PM's hierarchy is Governing Body → Club. AYSO's Area and Section layers don't have a structural representation, though regions can be grouped.
(no SC equivalent) Connected Systems The link between your Club System and the Governing System, set up from the Club System's Connected Systems menu. Team sync with Auto Rostering pushes rostered players and staff (with their photo and birth-certificate resources) to the Governing System as they are added; competition schedules can flow back. The pilot webinars called this "embedded registration"; the Help Center now says Connected Systems. ⚠️ "Program" and "Season" mean different things In SC, the Program was the top-level container and Season was a label. In PM, Season is the top-level container and Program sits inside it. Create the Season first — it sets date ranges and age classifications that Programs and Leagues inherit.
AYSO / SportsConnectPlayMetricsNotes
Program Program Same word, shifted meaning. In PM, a Program lives inside a Season. It's the registration container where you build packages, questions, and pricing.
Season Season In PM, the top-level container. Sets the date range and age classifications that all Programs and Leagues underneath inherit.
Division Package The most commonly confused mapping. A PM "package" defines the age group, gender, price, and linked questions — what families select at registration. Confirmed in the PM webinar: "Program Package = AYSO Division."
League (Area play) League Similar concept but PM uses it more broadly. In-house games and interregional play would be separate Leagues within the same Season.
Team Team Same concept. Created within Team Assignments or within a League.
Membership Year (MY26) Season AYSO's MY runs Aug 1–Jul 31 and you enter it when you create the Season. Your PM Season would span this period, or you'd create separate Fall and Spring seasons. The National Player Fee is paid once per Membership Year.

Volunteers & Staff

This is where terminology diverges most. SC treated everyone as a "volunteer" with a role. PM has a structured staff hierarchy with four designation types.

💡 The Four Staff Types in PlayMetrics Every volunteer must be categorized into one of these types before they can flow to the Governing System for background checks: Admin (board, RC, registrar), Director (division coordinators), Coach (assigned to a team), or Team Staff (everyone else — referees, team parents, etc.). Compliance & Background Checks

✅ Governing System Is Live — Sports Connect and Sports Affinity Are Being Retired Regions have the Governing System (purple interface) with JDP background screening and the AYSOU connection in place: past course completions were synced in and new ones arrive automatically. Volunteers reach AYSOU from their PlayMetrics Member role, not through Sports Connect, and new volunteers create their AYSOU account from inside PlayMetrics. Fingerprinting (LiveScan) is the one requirement still outside PlayMetrics. The full workflow is in the Volunteer Compliance Guide.

Common Gotchas

"I can't find Divisions"
In PM, divisions are called "packages." Look under your Program → Packages tab.
"Where's the Season setup?"
Create the Season first — it sets dates and age classifications. Then build Programs within it. The Season is the top-level container, which is the reverse of SC.
"Where do I put the National Player Fee?"
It is a Club Fee you create once per Membership Year (Financials → Additional Fees → Club Fees) and then select in each program's Additional Fees. Nothing arrives from AYSO National automatically, and a program with no club fee attached will open and collect money without it.
"Where are my volunteers?"
PM doesn't have a single "Volunteers" tab. Staff are split across Admins, Directors, Coaches, and Team Staff — each under the Staff menu. Volunteer interest captured by a program's Volunteer Roles exports from that program (Export Volunteers); League Volunteer Signups applications live under Leagues → your league → Volunteers tab (Needs Review / Accepted / All) with their own Export Volunteers. Compliance status is in the Governing System under Membership → Staff.
"How do I see all coaches and their teams?"
Export coaches from Staff → Coaches (includes team assignments). Or view League → Assignments for a visual drag-and-drop roster. Adding Coaches & Team Staff
"Where's the auto-team builder?"
It exists now, in beta: Auto Assign Players inside a team assignment for an in-house league drafts by age or tryout rating with team seeding and balancing. Ask success@playmetrics.com to turn it on. Until then (or for anything it can't express) use the drag-and-drop Team Assignment interface with player data, or export to Excel, use a tool like Bravaia, and re-import. Using Auto Assign
"Can I duplicate last year's setup?"
Yes — programs can be duplicated from one season to the next. Saves significant setup time after year one.
"When a season ends, do volunteers disappear?"
Similar to SC — you'll want a Season that spans Fall through Spring so volunteers remain visible. Confirmed in the webinar: "You will likely want to create a Season that extends the full length of Fall and Spring."
"What's a 'Director' — is that our Division Coordinator?"
Yes. PM's Director role maps to AYSO's Division Coordinator — oversight of a subset of teams. Account Types & Roles
"Can coaches see the admin notes/medical info?"
No — admin questions (like evaluations and medical notes) can be hidden from player contacts but visible to admins doing team assignments. Confirmed in the webinar: "the coaches will not see the notes."
