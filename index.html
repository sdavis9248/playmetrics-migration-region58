PlayMetrics Migration Resources

Reading this from another region? These guides were written for AYSO Region 58's own SportsConnect → PlayMetrics move, but most of the content applies to any AYSO region. Values that are Region 58 planning numbers rather than PlayMetrics limits are called out in the text, and anything marked CONFIRM IN-PRODUCT has not yet been verified in PlayMetrics — corrections are welcome. The Migration Status dashboard is Region 58's internal tracker. Share this site as playmetrics.ayso58.org.
